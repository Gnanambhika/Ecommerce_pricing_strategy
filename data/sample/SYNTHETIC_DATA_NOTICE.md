# Synthetic data notice

`synthetic_pricing_data.csv` and `messy_import_example.csv` in this folder are **synthetic**.

- They were generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` with a fixed random seed (42), so anyone can
  reproduce them exactly.
- All brands, products and sales channels are **fictional**. Any resemblance to real companies or
  marketplaces is coincidental.
- The numbers are **not real marketplace evidence** and must not be used to make claims about real
  shoppers, prices, competitors or profitability.
- The data was designed for teaching and demonstrating pricing analytics: demand follows a
  constant-elasticity model with a known true elasticity per category, plus seasonality, a festive peak,
  trend, competitor promotions, noise and occasional stock-outs.
- No website was scraped and no third-party data was used.

`messy_import_example.csv` is a deliberately corrupted sample (aliased headers, currency strings,
duplicates, invalid prices, bad dates) used to demonstrate the import validation workflow.
